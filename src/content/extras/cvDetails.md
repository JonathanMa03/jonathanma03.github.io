### CA-RNN Master's Thesis

- [Repository](https://github.com/JonathanMa03/innovative-droBVAR)
- Developed calibration-aware recurrent neural networks trained with joint
  negative log-likelihood, projected probability-integral-transform penalties,
  and temporal PIT moment penalties.
- Implemented frequentist and variational-Bayesian models with full-covariance
  Gaussian forecasts and documented theoretical limitations.
- Benchmarked RNN, bidirectional RNN, CA-RNN, and CA-BRNN models against
  historical, VAR, and VAR-GARCH baselines across simulated data-generating
  processes and distribution shifts.
- Packaged the work as a PyTorch library with chronological splits,
  deterministic seeds, checkpoints, experiment logs, resumable runs, and tests.
- Used a frozen confirmatory protocol, SHA-256 checks, moving-block bootstrap,
  CSV/NPZ artifacts, and negative-result reporting to support reproducibility.

### Modern AutoCriteria

- Dates: May 2026–Present
- [Repository](https://github.com/JonathanMa03/modern-autocrit)
- Migrated the system from LangChain to the OpenAI SDK and added structured
  logging, token accounting, and rate-limit retry behavior.
- Implemented ICD-10 and RxNorm normalization.
- Built a clinically aware distance metric that combines human judgment with an
  LLM-as-a-judge to distinguish superficially similar but contradictory
  criteria, such as “age greater than 60” and “age less than 60.”
- Developed a validation agent against ClinicalTrials.gov XML, reaching 91.8%
  exact match and 0.96 F1.
- Added dictionary and model-integration tests, structured exports, a CLI, and a
  Tkinter interface.

### DiffInEye

- [Repository](https://github.com/JonathanMa03/diff-in-eye)
- Framed OCT inpainting as an inverse problem with reproducible preprocessing,
  corruption, classical baselines, and diffusion models.
- Compared zero-fill, Gaussian, PDE, and Telea approaches against conditional
  encoder-decoder and U-Net DDPM models.
- Added deterministic preprocessing, checkpointing, metadata capture, schedule
  validation, masked losses, and benchmark scripts.
- Evaluated MSE, MAE, PSNR, retinal-layer continuity, and anatomical
  plausibility; Telea inpainting outperformed the diffusion approach in the
  reported experiments.
