\projectentry
{Interpretable Gene Programs or Foundation Models?}
{\href{https://github.com/JonathanMa03/gene-complexity}{GitHub}}
\begin{tightitemize}
\item Conducted a comparative study of processed gene-expression features, sparse dictionary learning, and pretrained scGPT representations for single-cell RNA-sequencing classification using 8,506 human pancreatic cells across ten cell types, evaluating all representations through a common L2-regularized multinomial logistic regression framework.
\item Evaluated predictive performance using Macro-F1, balanced accuracy, and multiclass log loss, demonstrating that a 64-atom sparse dictionary achieved a 31.25-fold reduction in feature dimensionality while retaining a held-out Macro-F1 of 0.9810, compared with 0.9939 for processed expression and 0.9624 for a custom pretrained scGPT checkpoint.
\item Conducted retrospective nested donor-held-out validation with matched gene availability, feature standardization, and controlled dictionary initialization to investigate generalization across biological donors and the effects of representation scaling on classifier regularization.
\item Applied convex optimization theory, Karush--Kuhn--Tucker (KKT) optimality conditions, Gram matrix eigenvalue analysis, and projected-gradient diagnostics to assess sparse-coding accuracy, conditional uniqueness, and the limitations of joint dictionary optimization.
\item Investigated the interpretability and reproducibility of learned gene programs through sign-aligned atom matching, cosine similarity, gene-weight overlap, and cell-type-specific activation patterns, distinguishing predictive stability from biological identifiability.
\end{tightitemize}

\projectentry
{CA-RNN (Master's Thesis)}
{\href{https://github.com/JonathanMa03/innovative-droBVAR}{GitHub}}
\begin{tightitemize}
\item Developed the Calibration-Aware Recurrent Neural Network (CA-RNN), a multivariate probabilistic forecasting model that augments joint negative log likelihood with differentiable projected-PIT and temporal PIT-moment penalties to target distributional and sequential calibration during training.
\item Derived frequentist and variational Bayesian formulations with full-covariance Gaussian predictive heads, then established theoretical limits by proving finite-criterion non-identification, constructing dependent uniform PITs with zero covariance, and relating proper-score regret to quantities outside the calibration loss.
\item Evaluated RNN, BRNN, CA-RNN, and CA-BRNN across controlled data-generating processes, market regimes, distribution shifts, ablations, and a disjoint external asset panel against historical, VAR, and VAR--GARCH probabilistic benchmarks.
\item Packaged the model as an installable PyTorch library with modular data, training, prediction, baseline, and evaluation interfaces supporting configurable CPU/GPU execution and reproducible application to new multivariate time-series panels.
\item Engineered leakage-safe chronological splits, deterministic training/projection/forecast seeds, matched initializations, validation checkpointing, epoch-level loss logging, resumable artifacts, and automated unit tests.
\item Established a frozen confirmatory protocol with SHA-256 verification, prespecified multi-seed moving-block bootstrap inference, structured CSV/NPZ exports, documented notebooks, and transparent reporting of negative findings and numerical failures.
